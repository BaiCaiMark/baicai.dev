Use and limitations

Yoru Gmail is a private assistant operated by its owner for the owner's own mailbox. These pages offer information, not account registration or a commercial service.

Authorized scope

Only the owner authorizes Google access. The integration requests Gmail read-only access and uses the owner's existing restricted NAS document tools and private Telegram chat. It does not authorize sending or modifying mail, external sharing of attachments, submitting forms, or changing NAS permissions.

Review important information

Email can be malicious, incomplete, or misleading. A matching sender address or display name is not independent proof of authenticity. AI summaries can contain errors and do not replace the original email, professional advice, or the owner's judgment. Check deadlines and required actions against the original source.

Availability and stopping

Monitoring depends on the NAS, network, Google authorization and APIs, the existing model service, and Telegram. Delays, authorization expiry, and partial failures can occur. This personal project provides no availability guarantee. The owner can stop monitoring or revoke Google access; existing stored copies are handled as described in the privacy policy.
