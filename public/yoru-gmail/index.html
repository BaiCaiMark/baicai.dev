Yoru Gmail

A private email assistant for its owner. This Desktop OAuth application is not offered for public signup or distribution.

What it does

Yoru checks the owner's connected Gmail account for new messages, identifies potentially important immigration correspondence, summarizes relevant messages in Traditional Chinese, saves permitted original attachments to the owner's private NAS, and sends a notification to the owner's existing private Telegram chat.

Read-only Google access

The only requested Google permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. Google grants read access to the connected mailbox and attachments; Yoru's own rules limit routine processing to relevant new mail. It cannot send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Where processing happens

Monitoring and attachment storage run on the owner's NAS. Selected, bounded message content is sent to the existing OpenAI-powered assistant for interpretation. Summaries and processing results are delivered through Telegram. The website itself does not connect to Gmail or receive Google credentials.

Control and limitations

The owner can stop monitoring or revoke Google access at any time. Sender identity is not assumed from a display name. Summaries can be incomplete or incorrect: verify important dates and instructions against the original email. Yoru does not reply, submit documents, or make immigration decisions.
